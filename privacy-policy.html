Privacy Policy

Updated: 10 November 2026

Prospect Flows is a UK-based sole trader business. This policy describes how personal information submitted through the website demonstration is processed and how optional website analytics are used. Our aim is to collect only the information needed for the enquiry and demonstration.

Who we are

Prospect Flows is the data controller for the website demonstration enquiry. For privacy questions or requests, contact admin@prospect-flows.com.

What you provide

When you use the demonstration form, we receive your name, email address and, if entered, business name. The submission may also be associated with technical and delivery information required to process the form and send the acknowledgement. We do not request payment details or special-category personal information through this form.

Why we use this information

We use the details you submit to run the demonstration, automatically acknowledge your enquiry, provide access to the Revenue at Risk Calculator, and respond to any related enquiry. The form includes a required consent checkbox. Any separate B2B prospecting activity is distinct from this demonstration and should not be confused with consent to receive marketing emails.

How the demonstration works

The website form submits to Web3Forms and then uses connected email and automation services to route and respond to the submission. The email acknowledgement is part of the product demonstration. Submission of this form is not a request for a marketing mailing list.

Where information may be held

Prospect Flows does not intend to build a separate mailing list or customer database from demonstration submissions. Nevertheless, submitted details are necessarily processed by Web3Forms, the connected delivery and automation services, and email systems involved in the acknowledgement. These services may retain submission or delivery records under their own settings and retention policies. We cannot accurately describe the demonstration as involving no processing or no records.

Optional analytics

We use Google Analytics only if you select “Allow analytics” in the website preference notice. If you reject optional analytics, the Google Analytics script is not loaded by the site. Your preference is stored in your browser. The calculator works without analytics, and its entered commercial estimates are calculated in your browser; they are not sent to Google Analytics as calculator event details by this website.

Data retention and requests

We aim not to retain demonstration data longer than needed for the enquiry and any necessary operational, security or legal purposes. Records processed through third-party services may remain subject to those services’ retention settings. You may contact us to ask what information we hold and request correction, access or deletion where applicable.

Your rights

Under applicable UK data protection law, you may have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, objection and withdrawal of consent. You can raise concerns with the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO).

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. We use access controls and service-level safeguards appropriate to the enquiry and demonstration, but no internet-based process can promise absolute security.

Changes

We may update this policy when our processing arrangements change. The updated version will be published here.
